Our History

Ambady hails from the ancient Ambady Oil Mill, established in 1946 by Mr. Gangadharan. Decades of trust, entrepreneurial grit and several generations of business evolution shape everything we do today.

  1. 1946

    Mr. Gangadharan establishes the Ambady Oil Mill.

  2. Generations

    Decades of trust and entrepreneurial grit carry the business forward.

  3. Today

    Ambady Business Corporation spans engineering, trading, manpower, compliance, resorts and boat clubs.

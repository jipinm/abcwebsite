Project Management Consultancy

Specialised engineering consultancy for oil and gas projects, covering technical supervision from planning to commissioning.

We supply specialised drilling and industrial materials, including Bentonite and Barodite drilling lubrication powder.

Manpower Resources & Mining Recruitment

Global recruitment and deployment of highly qualified technical manpower, safety personnel and specialised engineers.

Document Reference & Technical Compliance

Auditing, compliance assurance and HSE documentation, including HIRA, JSA and PTW systems.

Our procedural frameworks align with international standards.

Resorts & Hotels Hospitality

We own and operate three boutique resorts at the tourist destinations of Kovalam, Varkala and Thiruvallam.

We also run two-star, multi-storey hotels at Kovalam Havva Beach.

Ambady Boat Clubs

Ambady Boat Club stands near the Punnamada Finishing Point in Alappuzha, home of traditional Kerala water sports and boat races.

A second club is in process in the Trivandrum district.
